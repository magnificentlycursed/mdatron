# MDATRON-E0092 — reserved-word-misuse

**Severity:** error
**Status:** accepted
**Introduced in:** 0.2.0

## What this means

A reserved-status term appears in governed prose. Reserved means held for a registered future sense — not yet usable. Whether a given use is inside the registered sense cannot be judged mechanically, so every use is surfaced for review; the registry entry's sense field says what the term is being held for.

## How to fix

Remove or rephrase the use, or — if the term's time has come — promote it to registered status in a reviewed commit.
